A few helpful answers

How do I add a book?

Use the import control on the library screen and select a book in the system Files picker. QuietPage keeps its own copy. Importing the exact same file again does not add another copy.

Which files can I read?

QuietPage supports DRM-free, reflowable EPUB 2 and EPUB 3 books. PDF, fixed-layout EPUB, and DRM-protected books are not supported. QuietPage does not sell or supply books.

Can I read offline?

Once a book has been imported, its core reading features work locally. Opening an external link uses the system browser and may require an internet connection.

How does read aloud work?

If your installed version includes Read aloud, open it from the reading menu and choose Apple speech or your own ElevenLabs model and voice. ElevenLabs needs a valid API key, network access for uncached audio, and your confirmation before sending text to the service. Its account charges and data policies apply. See the privacy policy for details.

Can I pause speech with headphones?

Compatible headset controls can pause and resume active narration. Disconnecting the audio output pauses narration. Hardware controls depend on the headset and connection. The on-screen and lock-screen controls are also available.

Why can speech pause between sentences?

Uncached cloud audio must be generated before it can play. QuietPage reuses generated audio and prefetches a limited amount, but generation speed and network conditions can still cause waiting. Check your account allowance and connection if an error is shown, or choose Apple speech.

How do I change the reading appearance?

Open Reading settings from the reading menu to change the font, size, line spacing, margins, alignment, or theme. Reading preferences are shared across books. Light, Paper, and Dark themes are also available from the library Settings menu.

Where are my books and reading positions stored?

Imported copies, reading positions, bookmarks, and settings are stored in the app on your device. QuietPage has no account system or built-in cloud sync.

What happens when I delete a book?

After confirmation, QuietPage removes its imported copy and the associated reading progress, bookmarks, and speech cache. Your original source file is left untouched. Importing it again adds it as a new book.

How do I report a problem?

Email support with a description, the steps that led to the problem, your iOS version, and your QuietPage version. After returning to the library, Settings can export diagnostic logs from your latest reading session. Sharing them is optional; review the file before sending it. Please do not send copyrighted book files.
